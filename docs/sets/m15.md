# Magic 2015 census

The Magic 2015 (M15) cards not yet in the pool, and which wait on a
capability. Derived 2026-10-27 from MTGJSON's set list against `data/cards/`.
The set list is `data/decks/sets/m15.txt`, which the suite reads, so every card
of the set stays in the pool.

## Needs a capability

Nothing. Every Magic 2015 card is in the pool.

## How it went

Finished 2026-10-29.

- Filed along the way: #5711 (planeswalker loyalty in `parun translate`;
  measured, not landed), #5725 (dying creatures triggering nothing,
  Hushbringer), #5733 (mana abilities record no activation), #5740 (a
  departed player's casts drop out of spell tallies). Follow-up PR: #5748

- Audits: 10 of 11 capability PRs, plus hand batch #5730; 3 found rules
  defects. #5750 was skipped at the orchestrator's judgement (one cost
  constructor, one card re-encoded).
  - #5723: a Chain Veil grant was spent by any player's activation, not only
    its holder's.
  - #5734: a copy trigger copied nothing when the original ability was
    countered while it waited.
  - #5739: a departed opponent's life loss was not counted (the
    `docs/decisions.md` entry the unit added was forced the other way by
    rulings, and was dropped).
- Subagent tokens: census 389k; hand 461k, 438k; capability 357k (#5723),

  translator spike (#5711) cost 118k more. Wall clock ~40h including a pause
